What the platform will do

Four features, one goal: less time between danger and help

These are the core features planned for the Soshanguve Community Forum app and website. They are designed around how our members already communicate, so the platform feels familiar from day one rather than replacing what already works.

Service 01

Real-time crime alerts

When a member reports a threat, every nearby profile receives an alert immediately — no need to wait for a phone call chain or a whistle to be heard down the street. This directly addresses the biggest weakness in our current system: warnings that arrive too late to the right people.

Planned

Service 02

Incident reporting

Members will be able to log what happened, where, and when directly in the app — safely, and without needing to confront a suspect or gather in person. This matters because community patrollers have been seriously hurt doing exactly that kind of in-person response in Soshanguve before.

Planned

Service 03

Community announcements

Forum organisers can post safety updates, upcoming imbizo dates, or general notices to every member at once — replacing scattered WhatsApp forwards with one reliable place everyone checks.

Planned

Service 04

Emergency button with live location tracking

A single button a member can press when they are in immediate danger. Pressing it shares their live location with nearby volunteers and emergency contacts, similar in spirit to the panic-button pilots government has already tested in parts of Soshanguve — but built specifically around our own Forum network.

Planned

Access

Free, and built for low-data reality

Every feature above is being planned with one constraint front of mind: many of our members cannot always afford data or airtime. The platform will remain free to use, and future versions will aim to keep data use as light as possible, so the tool works for the members who need it most — not just those who can already afford existing options.
