Community safety, built by the community

When something happens in Soshanguve, everyone should hear about it in seconds — not hours.

The Soshanguve Community Forum is a group of local residents who already look out for one another using phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, and whistles. This website is the start of a free, accessible safety platform built to help that same community respond faster, with less risk to the volunteers who step up every day.

Why this matters

Soshanguve needs a faster way to warn each other

Crime in parts of Soshanguve, especially the Jukulyn section, has been described by Gauteng officials as alarming, with police recording hundreds of active cases at once. Government has already rolled out panic-button pilots and CCTV in some blocks because existing communication — word of mouth, phone calls, whistles — simply isn't fast enough in a real emergency. The Forum's members live this every day, and many can't always afford the data or airtime needed to use those pilot tools.
